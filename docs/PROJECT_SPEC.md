# Buildans Gas Suppliers - Project Spec
Last updated: 2026-10-18

## Purpose
Business context, users, coverage, offerings, 3-tap flow.

## Business
LPG refills (cylinder swap), new cylinder sales, accessories (hoses, regulators, burners, cooker tops), door-to-door ~30 min.

## Coverage
Ngoingwa (hub), Section 9, Makongeni, Kiboko, Landless, Thika Town, Blue Post, Garissa Road + surrounding.

## Users
Estate residents on mid-range Android phones over 3G/4G. Want to reorder in 3 taps or fewer, no account/sign-up.

## 3-Tap Flow
1. Pick estate (or keep default)
2. Choose refill/new set + configure
3. Enter phone + house ? confirm & dispatch
